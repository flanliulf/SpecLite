# Brainstorming Facilitation

Facilitate a structured brainstorming session through multi-turn conversation until the problem, audience, constraints, and desired output are clear.

## Process

1. **Frame the challenge** - Clarify the decision, opportunity, or problem to explore.
2. **Set boundaries** - Identify constraints, stakeholders, success criteria, and evidence already available.
3. **Diverge** - Generate multiple candidate directions, including conservative, ambitious, and unconventional options.
4. **Converge** - Group ideas, identify patterns, remove weak duplicates, and surface trade-offs.
5. **Stress-test** - Challenge assumptions, risks, user impact, feasibility, and differentiation.
6. **Package** - Summarize the strongest options, open questions, and recommended next steps.

## Output

A structured brainstorming summary with candidate ideas, rationale, risks, and follow-up actions.
